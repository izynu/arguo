Be respectful. Disagreement about code, sources, and methods is normal and useful; contempt for people is not.

Expected behavior: use welcoming and inclusive language, stay on topic in project spaces, accept critique of work without making it personal, and give credit where it is due.

Unacceptable behavior: harassment of any kind, personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, publishing private information about others without consent, trolling or sustained disruption, and sexualized language or unwelcome attention.

Scope. This code applies to project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat channels, and any event where a person represents the project. It also applies when representing the project externally, for example in talks or third-party threads about the toolkit.

Reporting. Report problems privately to the maintainers by email or by contacting a maintainer directly. The reporting address is a placeholder marked TODO for the repository owner until launch and will be listed on the support and security pages before the first public announcement. Reports are handled confidentially as far as practical, and retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

Enforcement. Maintainers may warn, temporarily mute, or permanently remove a participant from project spaces. Severe or repeated harassment leads to permanent removal. Enforcement decisions are recorded privately, with a public summary when that does not expose anyone involved.

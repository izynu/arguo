## Evidence hierarchies

Not all studies answer a question equally well. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses sit above single trials; randomized controlled trials sit above cohort and case-control studies; case reports and expert opinion sit at the bottom. The hierarchy is a starting point, not a verdict: a large well conducted cohort study can outweigh a small sloppy trial, and a meta-analysis is only as good as its inclusion criteria.

Registries are the check on the literature. Comparing a published trial against its ClinicalTrials.gov or ICTRP entry exposes outcome switching and unpublished results, and checking PROSPERO shows whether a review was registered before it ran and whether it drifted from its protocol.

For consumer facing claims, MedlinePlus and NICE give careful summaries, but the load-bearing citation for a clinical claim should still reach the study or the review that established it.
